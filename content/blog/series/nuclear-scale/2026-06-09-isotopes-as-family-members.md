Atoms of the same element always have the same number of protons, but their neutron counts can differ. That makes isotopes a useful test of what changes an atom's identity and what changes only its mass.

Learners build isotope cards from two colors of counters. They group cards by proton number, order them by total nucleon count, and mark which combinations are stable or unstable.

The resulting table turns a vocabulary lesson into a classification problem that students can explain using a model they assembled themselves.
