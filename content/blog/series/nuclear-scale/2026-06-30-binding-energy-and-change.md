Nuclear reactions rearrange protons and neutrons into configurations with different binding energies. The energy released is tied to the small difference between the mass of the starting system and the mass of the products.

A classroom energy-accounting model can compare separated pieces, a bound system, and the energy carried away during the change. The aim is not a full calculation but a consistent story about where the energy comes from.

This final step connects decay, fission, and fusion through one idea: nuclear structure changes, and the complete energy balance includes both matter and radiation.
