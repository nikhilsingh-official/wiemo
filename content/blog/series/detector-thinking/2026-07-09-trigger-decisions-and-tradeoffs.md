Trigger logic gives students a concrete way to discuss speed, selectivity, missed events, and why more data is not always more useful. This is intentionally the densest filler post in the new series set.

The classroom simulation runs quickly. Cards move past a team, each with partial event information. Students must decide which cards to keep before seeing the full explanation on the back.

The debrief compares conservative and aggressive rules. It asks which discoveries each strategy protected, which ordinary cases it collected, and which rare cases it may have thrown away too soon.
