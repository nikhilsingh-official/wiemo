A detector becomes easier to reason about when students can assign each layer a role, limitation, and kind of evidence. This high-complexity filler post gives the CMS a dense technical path.

The model starts with a tracker, then adds calorimeter-like layers, then introduces a final outer layer for particles that travel farther. Students do not need full detector engineering; they need the logic of why one layer is not enough.

The post can later become a full lesson plan with diagrams, event cards, and a reflection prompt about how scientific instruments distribute trust across multiple measurements.
