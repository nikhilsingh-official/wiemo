Students often arrive with big questions. The first move is helping them reshape wonder into a testable classroom-scale experiment without flattening the curiosity that made the question interesting.

This sample lab asks learners to move from "why does this happen" to "what could we change, what could we measure, and what would count as evidence." The facilitator writes three possible variables on the board and lets small groups choose one.

The post can later hold student examples, photos of planning sheets, and the short reflection prompts that help learners explain why their question is testable.
