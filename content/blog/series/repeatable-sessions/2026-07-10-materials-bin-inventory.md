A shared inventory makes it easier to notice what is missing before the team is already on the way to a session. This post can stand in for a practical note about keeping workshop kits ready.

The sample bin includes measuring tape, magnets, index cards, markers, clips, batteries, small bulbs, rubber bands, string, and a few backup notebooks. Each item can be marked as ready, low, missing, or replaced.

The useful part is the habit. When the same inventory language appears in every post, the team can compare sessions without reconstructing the basics from memory.
