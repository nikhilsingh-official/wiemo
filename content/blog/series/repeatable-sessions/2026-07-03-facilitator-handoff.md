A handoff post captures what the plan said, what the room did, and what a future facilitator should change. This placeholder keeps the tone practical and low complexity.

The format can be short: session goal, strongest prompt, confusing moment, timing drift, material issue, and one recommendation for next time. Those notes are small enough to write immediately after the visit.

Over time, handoffs become a quiet source of quality control. The team can see which activities are stable and which ones need another design pass.
