Before a session starts, the room setup often decides whether the first ten minutes feel calm or improvised. This filler post gives editors a practical operations note that can become a repeatable template later.

The checklist starts with table layout, sight lines, power access, projector position, and a small materials station near the facilitator. It also leaves space for school-specific constraints, because the same activity rarely lands in the same room twice.

The point is not to freeze the session. It is to remove the predictable friction so facilitators can spend their attention on students instead of furniture.
