Before students see a CMS-style display, they can practice reconstructing a hidden event from marks left on ordinary materials. This makes the abstract idea of traces feel concrete.

Small groups receive paper with trails, dots, gaps, and smudges. They propose what might have moved through the space, what changed direction, and what evidence is too weak to support a claim.

Only after that warm-up does the facilitator show a detector image. The classroom trace activity gives students a vocabulary for uncertainty before the physics vocabulary arrives.
