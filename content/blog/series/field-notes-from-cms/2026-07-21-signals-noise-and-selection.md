A detector sees many events, so students need a concrete way to think about selection instead of imagining discovery as a single perfect picture. This post sketches that bridge.

The classroom version uses a deck of event cards. Each card has visible features, missing information, and a small amount of irrelevant detail. Groups create selection rules, test them, and then compare which interesting cases their rules missed.

That discussion opens the door to false positives, false negatives, thresholds, and the careful tradeoffs that make experimental physics more than a search for dramatic images.
