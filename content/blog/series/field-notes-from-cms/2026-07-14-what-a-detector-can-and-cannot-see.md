Students understand measurements better when the lesson includes what the instrument misses, filters, or only infers indirectly. This placeholder gives the CMS series a post about limits.

The session compares direct observation with inferred evidence. Learners look at simple tracks, hidden causes, and measurements that only make sense when several detector layers are combined.

The facilitator can use the post to collect analogies that work: footprints, shadows, receipt totals, phone location logs, or any familiar trace that reveals an event without showing the event itself.
