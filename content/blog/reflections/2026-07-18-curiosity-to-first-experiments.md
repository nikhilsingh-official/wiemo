Physics starts to feel real when students can touch the question. Instead of opening with definitions, this placeholder story frames the workshop around observations: what changes, what stays stable, and what could be measured with simple tools.

The first activity asks learners to sketch their assumptions before the demonstration begins. Those sketches become useful evidence later, because students can compare what they expected with what the experiment actually showed.

For the CMS, this kind of post can become a lightweight field note: what happened in the room, what students noticed, and what the team would change before running the session again.
